# Thumbnail style notes

From Jacob's 5 reference thumbnails from *Throw Food for Animals* (11 Nov 2026). Use these rules for every Score on Animals thumbnail and icon prompt (RoThumbs).

## Layout
- One formula: the player (bacon-hair avatar, smug grin) is small, on the left or in the foreground. The target is huge, on the right. One action links them.
- You get the game in half a second: player, action trail, animal.
- The animal is the hero, about 40-50% of the frame. Always a top-tier creature (Dragon, T-Rex, Phoenix, rainbow animal). Never a common.
- Curiosity hook: a black silhouette with a white "?" and red "!!" makes people click.
- Variety without changing the idea: mostly side view. One over-the-shoulder shot from behind the player. One shows progression (biome steps grass > desert > lava > purple leading to the creature).

## Text
- 3 of 5 have no text. The picture carries it.
- Never the game title.
- At most one short line: a rarity or value number ("1 IN 999B") or a name + $/s ("PHOENIX $68,543B/S").
- Top-right corner, never over the player, the item or the animal.
- Chunky rounded font, thick dark outline, rainbow gradient fill (or white + green for money).
- Numbers are absurdly big on purpose: bait, not explanation.

## Effects
- One main effect per thumbnail: the throw trail (white streak or yellow arc) from the hand to the target.
- Support only: speed lines under a leaping animal, soft round ground shadows, 1-2 cartoon clouds, the "?" / "!!" on mystery shots.
- None of: particles, sparkles, glow, light rays, lens flares, explosions, blurred backgrounds, dramatic lighting. Flat, bright light.
- Reason: it stays readable as a tiny mobile tile.

## Look
- Thick outlines on everything; bright, saturated colours that pop off the background.
- Simple backgrounds: blue sky + a couple of clouds + flat stud grass, or the game's own map.
- Everything in the in-game stud / blocky style.

## For Score on Animals
- Action: the player kicking the ball (big arc or trail) at a huge rare egg keeper diving in the goal, or a black-silhouette mystery animal with "?" in goal.
- Text: optional rarity line top-right, e.g. "1 IN 10M" in rainbow, or a Secret animal's name + $/s.
- Background: our stadium, goal and crowd. Not the dirt walls with dripping grass: those look almost identical to Throw Food for Animals.
